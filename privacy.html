Privacy Policy

Igor's Forge · last updated September 24, 2026

Igor's Forge is a desktop application used only by its owner to run the Failure Envelope YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a Google account, the app requests access to YouTube data for that account: channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API) and, where enabled, uploading the owner's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner their channel's performance and to publish the owner's own videos.
  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Igor's Forge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: via the contact details on the Failure Envelope YouTube channel's About page.
